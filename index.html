ABOUT

Structure is a form of care for the people doing the work.

I love the messy middle — the space between a big idea and a delivered outcome. My work is about creating clarity: designing the SOPs, timelines, governance, and rituals that let talented people do their best work without friction.

Over the last few years I have led delivery for a digital healthcare product, built the operational foundation of Web3 education communities, recruited and onboarded founding teams, and run high-impact events from concept to completion.

I move comfortably across industries — healthcare, Web3, AI, education, and events — because the fundamentals travel: understand the goal, map the system, align the people, and track what matters.

I came to this work sideways — a chemistry degree, not a traditional PM path — and built my way in through real delivery. Now I teach project management at IOTB Tech and TGM Academy, because the same systems that worked for me can work for someone else starting from a different place.
